We offer three levels of courses:
  • Beginner - Ideal for those with no prior trading experience. Covers forex fundamentals market structure, and trading basics.
  • Intermediate - For traders with basic knowledge looking to refine strategy. Covers technical & fundamental analysis, risk management, and psychology.
  • Advanced - For experienced traders. Focuses on instituional trading strategies, algorithmic trading, and risk adjusted portfolio management.
